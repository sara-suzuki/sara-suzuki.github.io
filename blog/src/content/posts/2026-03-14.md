Welcome! I'm excited to launch this blog as a space to share research updates, reflections on the work, and resources for anyone interested in youth civic engagement, critical consciousness, and building a more just democracy.

I'll use this space to:

- Share early findings and behind-the-scenes looks at ongoing research
- Reflect on what it means to do equity-centered work inside institutions
- Highlight resources and tools that practitioners and researchers might find useful
- Write more personally about the questions that animate my work

Thank you for being here. I look forward to the conversation.
